Our Story

How Scentonish Was Born

Finding authentic, premium fragrances in Pakistan has always been challenging. Counterfeit products, unreliable sellers, and no single trusted source made it nearly impossible for fragrance lovers to access genuine luxury scents.

In 2024, Junaid decided to change this. He built direct relationships with international fragrance distributors to bring authentic, high-quality perfumes to Pakistan at fair prices—all with the simplicity of ordering on WhatsApp and paying cash on delivery.

Today, Scentonish offers carefully curated fragrances for men, women, and unisex collections. Every bottle is genuine, tested, and approved. We're not just selling perfumes; we're sharing passion for great scents.
