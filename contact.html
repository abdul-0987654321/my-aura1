Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order? ▼

Simply WhatsApp us at +92 345 7566768! Tell us the fragrance name and your delivery address. We'll confirm stock, share the price, and arrange Cash on Delivery.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery? ▼

Yes! COD is available for all orders across Pakistan. You pay when the fragrance arrives at your door—no advance payment needed.

How long is shipping? ▼

Rawalpindi: Next day delivery. Other cities (Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, etc.): 3–5 business days. Express options also available—ask us on WhatsApp.

Are fragrances authentic? ▼

100% authentic. We source directly from trusted international distributors. Every bottle is genuine and comes with authenticity assurance. We stake our reputation on it.

What's your return policy? ▼

30-day return/exchange policy. If you're not satisfied, message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out immediately—no questions asked.

Can I track my order? ▼

Yes! Once your order ships, we'll send you a tracking number via WhatsApp so you can monitor your delivery in real-time.
