Policy Details

What Can & Cannot Be Returned

✅ Eligible for Return

  • Item received damaged or defective
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Item significantly different from description
  • Unused item in original condition within 30 days
  • Missing accessories or parts
  • Item stopped working within warranty period

❌ Not Eligible for Return

  • Item used and in good working condition
  • Damage caused by misuse or mishandling
  • Return requested after 30 days
  • Item without original packaging (may affect eligibility)
  • Consumable items once opened
  • Custom or personalized orders
